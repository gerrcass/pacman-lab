# PacMan

A browser remake of the arcade game Pac-Man: PacMan eats Pellets in a Maze
while four Ghosts hunt him, and Power Pellets flip the hunt.

## Language

**PacMan**:
The player-controlled character that moves through the Maze eating Pellets.
_Avoid_: Player, Pac-Man, hero

**Ghost**:
One of the four enemies that hunt PacMan through the Maze. Each Ghost has a
distinct targeting rule.
_Avoid_: Enemy, monster, spirit

**Blinky / Pinky / Inky / Clyde**:
The four Ghosts, distinguished by colour and targeting rule.
_Avoid_: red/blue/pink/orange ghost

**Maze**:
The fixed grid of walls and corridors the game is played on. Walls block
movement; corridors carry Pellets. A Maze is a layout, not a difficulty stage.
_Avoid_: Board, map, level

**Level**:
One playthrough of a Maze at a given difficulty. Clearing every Pellet
advances to the next Level.
_Avoid_: Stage, wave, round

**Pellet**:
A small dot in the Maze that PacMan eats for a small score. Clearing all of
them completes the Maze.
_Avoid_: Dot, coin, food

**Power Pellet**:
A large dot that, when eaten, turns the Ghosts Frightened for a short time.
_Avoid_: Energizer, power pill, big dot

**Scatter**:
The Ghost mode in which each Ghost retreats to its home corner of the Maze.
_Avoid_: Retreat, idle

**Chase**:
The Ghost mode in which each Ghost pursues PacMan by its targeting rule.
_Avoid_: Attack, hunt, pursuit

**Frightened**:
The Ghost mode, entered by eating a Power Pellet, in which Ghosts flee from
PacMan and can be eaten for points.
_Avoid_: Scared, vulnerable, blue mode

**Fruit**:
A bonus item that appears partway through a Maze and grants points when eaten.
_Avoid_: Bonus, cherry, prize
